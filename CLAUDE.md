# Cape Town Quiz

## About this project

A fun multiple choice quiz for visitors to Cape Town. While they play, visitors learn a lot about the city they are visiting: its history, nature, food, culture, languages, neighbourhoods and practical tips.

The full plan is in **PRD.md** (Product Requirements Document: what we build, for whom, and why).
